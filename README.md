# DocADAMS Pitch / Vision Site

This repository presents the current public vision, mission, goals, development status and team for **DocADAMS**.

DocADAMS is being built as a connected healthcare workflow platform rather than a standalone diagnostic chatbot. The product direction connects:

- longitudinal patient cases, visits and documents,
- patient-facing AI-assisted understanding and navigation,
- provider / facility discovery through DocADAMS MAP / DoctorMAP,
- appointments and authorized clinician workflows,
- doctor AI decision support with clinician review,
- and approved visits returning to the longitudinal patient record.

## Vision

Make connected, understandable and affordable healthcare infrastructure available beyond well-integrated health systems, especially in fragmented and underserved healthcare markets.

## Mission

Connect patient context, safe AI assistance, healthcare discovery and clinician workflows while preserving clinician authority, source provenance, affordability and global portability.

## Current phase

**C15 / DoctorMAP integration and hardening.**

The product is still in development and is not yet production-ready or formally clinically validated.
